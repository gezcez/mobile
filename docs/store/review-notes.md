# Notes for app review (App Store and Play)

Paste into "Notes" (App Review Information) and the Play "App access" section.

## English

gezcez is a free, non-profit transit app. No account is needed to review it: open the app,
choose a city (Kocaeli works best) and the Home tab shows nearby stops and departures. Location
permission is optional; you can pick a city by hand.

Signing in is optional and only syncs saved stops and lines. Test account: use the email
`review@test.gezcez.com` on the sign-in page; the one-time code is `<STAGING_TEST_OTP for the
review account>` (fill in before submitting).

Card balance: for networks whose card system offers a public balance check, riders can type the
number printed on their own transit card. The number is stored encrypted on the device and sent
only to our server, which asks the operator's public balance service and does not store it. gezcez
is not affiliated with the operators or card providers and shows no operator branding. If you do
not have a local card, the feature shows "Balance not checked yet" or "not supported".

Account deletion: Me, Delete account (opens the account page at /account/privacy/delete), or Me,
Privacy centre, Delete my data.

## Türkçe (Play iç inceleme notu)

gezcez ücretsiz, kâr amacı gütmeyen bir toplu taşıma uygulamasıdır. İnceleme için hesap gerekmez:
uygulamayı açın, bir şehir seçin (Kocaeli) ve Ana sayfa yakındaki durakları ve kalkışları gösterir.
Giriş isteğe bağlıdır ve yalnızca kayıtlı durak ve hatları eşitler. Kart numarası cihazda şifreli
saklanır, yalnızca bakiye sorgusu için sunucumuza gönderilir ve saklanmaz.
