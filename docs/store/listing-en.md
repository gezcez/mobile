## Description

gezcez is a free, independent transit app for every city it supports. Open it at the stop and see
when your bus comes and where it is.

- Nearby stops: your location stays on your phone; gezcez finds the stops around you on the device.
- Live departures: realtime times where the city publishes them, the timetable everywhere else.
- Live map: buses moving on the map, for a whole city or just your line.
- Saved stops and lines: group them by colour; sign in to keep them on all your devices.
- Transit cards: check your card balance and trips where the city's card system allows it. The
  card number is stored encrypted on your phone.
- Works offline with the stops, routes and departures you last saw.
- Türkçe and English, light and dark.

No account is needed. No ads and no tracking. gezcez counts how often features are used, per day
and city, with no IDs; you can switch that off in Settings.

gezcez is a non-profit project. It is not affiliated with any transit operator or card provider;
transit data is shown as each city publishes it and may be incomplete.
